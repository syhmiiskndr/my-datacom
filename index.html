1. Business Introduction

1.1 The Business

ByteNexus Cyber Café is a modern, high-performance digital hub strategically designed to bridge the gap between competitive gaming, casual browsing, and academic productivity. Operating from a single 15' × 25' (375 sq. ft.) commercial shop lot, the café provides:

  • High-speed fiber internet access (800 Mbps)
  • Premium gaming stations with RTX 4050 graphics
  • Essential network services (printing, scanning, file sharing)
  • Professional workspace for remote workers
  • Comfortable, community-driven environment

1.2 Location

The café is strategically located in Durian Tunggal, Malacca, positioned directly near student residential zones and commercial areas. The compact 15' × 25' footprint is optimally designed to maximize workstation density while maintaining strict compliance with local safety and ventilation standards. Proximity to university campuses ensures consistent customer traffic from the primary demographic.

1.3 Target Market

University Students

Primary demographic requiring stable, low-latency connections for online assessments, collaborative group projects, and multiplayer gaming.

Esports Enthusiasts

Individuals seeking high-end hardware capable of running modern AAA and esports titles smoothly with sub-15ms latency.

Remote Workers & Freelancers

Professionals needing temporary workspace with reliable internet, printing facilities, and a professional environment.

Casual Gamers

Individuals looking for a social gaming environment with powerful systems and community atmosphere.

3. Proposed Network Topology & Design

3.1 Topology Overview

The network utilizes a Physical and Logical Star Topology. Every single device (Client PCs, Admin PC, Printer, Access Point, Server) connects via an individual point-to-point cable back to a centralized Cisco 24-Port Switch serving as the single point of distribution.

3.2 Topology Justification

Star topology ensures that if a single Cat6 cable or client network interface card fails, only that specific station goes offline. The rest of the cyber café remains completely operational, minimizing business downtime and simplifying troubleshooting. This is critical for a commercial operation where reliability directly impacts revenue and customer satisfaction.

3.5 Communication Media

Wired Media

Category 6 (Cat6) Unshielded Twisted Pair (UTP) cables are used for all fixed assets (PCs, Printer, Server). Cat6 easily supports data transfer speeds up to 1 Gbps at 100 meters, mitigates crosstalk, and provides optimal ping latency (< 5ms) for gaming applications.

Wireless Media

IEEE 802.11ac (Wi-Fi 5/6) broadcasted via the ceiling-mounted Access Point specifically for mobile devices belonging to waiting customers. Provides flexible connectivity without permanent cabling requirements.

4.3 VLSM & Segmentation Strategy

Management Pool (192.168.10.1–192.168.10.30): Reserved for critical infrastructure and administrative systems. Assigned with static IPs for reliability and easy identification.

Gaming Client Pool (192.168.10.31–192.168.10.42): Reserved DHCP range for the 12 gaming workstations. Reserved DHCP ensures consistency while allowing ease of management through the café management software.

Customer Wi-Fi Pool (192.168.10.100–192.168.10.200): Dynamic DHCP range for guests and customers using mobile devices. Isolated from gaming infrastructure to prevent interference and ensure optimal bandwidth allocation for paying gaming customers.

5.2 Justification & Analysis

Why Fiber Optic Technology?

Fiber optic infrastructure offers symmetrical/high-speed throughput unaffected by weather conditions or line congestion. Unlike copper-based Streamyx (affected by distance, weather, and congestion), fiber maintains consistent performance.

Performance for Gaming

An 800 Mbps pipe guarantees that even if 4 clients are simultaneously downloading 50GB game patches or streaming 4K content, the remaining 8 gaming clients will experience stable latency under 15ms to local gaming servers. This is crucial for competitive gaming where sub-20ms ping is mandatory.

Additional Benefits

Includes a Fixed Static IP vital for remote network management, hosting secure local gaming lobbies, and enabling port-forwarding for specialized gaming services. Provides 24/7 technical support suitable for commercial operations.

6. Conclusion

The proposed network design for ByteNexus Cyber Café effectively addresses all operational requirements specified within the 15' × 25' commercial constraints. By integrating a centralized Star topology using robust Cat6 UTP cabling, structured Class C IP allocation with VLSM segmentation, enterprise-grade Cisco and Ubiquiti networking equipment, and Unifi Business Fibre internet access, the business is guaranteed a:

  • Scalable Network Architecture: Star topology supports future expansion with minimal reconfiguration
  • Reliable & Redundant Infrastructure: UPS-backed critical systems ensure uptime even during power interruptions
  • High-Performance Gaming Environment: Sub-15ms latency and 1Gbps LAN speeds suitable for competitive play
  • Professional Management: DHCP pools, static infrastructure IPs, and managed switching for easy administration
  • Security-First Design: Firewall, URL filtering, VLAN segmentation, and monitored access control
  • Cost-Effective Operations: Total startup of RM 64,800 with sustainable monthly operating costs

This foundational network design meets all structural, financial, technical, and operational standards, presenting a viable, professional business proposal ready for immediate deployment. The integration of modern networking standards, enterprise-grade hardware, and strategic internet selection ensures ByteNexus can compete effectively in the cybercafé market while maintaining operational excellence and customer satisfaction.
